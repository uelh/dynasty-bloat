[h1] Dynasty Bloat [/h1]
This mod limits how many children each member of your dynasty can have.

Large dynasties become a chore. Each child needs an education, then a marriage. Then their children need the same, and the work grows with every generation. This mod sets a limit. When a character reaches the limit, the mod gives them a trait and they stop having children. 

[h1] What the mod does [/h1]
[list]
[*]Counts the children of every member of your dynasty.
[*]Gives a character the Settled trait once they reach the limit, so they stop having children.
[*]Recounts after every birth and every death, so the trait comes and goes as your family changes.
[/list]

[h1] Game rules [/h1]
Both rules sit in the Dynasty Bloat and Tweaks categories. You can change either one at the start of a game:

[list]
[*][b]Dynasty Bloat: Child Limit[/b] — the number of children allowed. Choose Off, 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5. The default is [b]3[/b].
[*][b]Dynasty Bloat: Child Count[/b] — what counts. [i]Living children[/i] means a child who dies no longer counts, and this is the default. [i]Children ever born[/i] means a child who dies still counts.
[/list]


[h1] Limitations [/h1]
[list]
[*] Pregnancies already underway and twins can bypass the limit.
[*] The game checks whether a character can have children when it arranges marriages. A character at the limit is less likely to be matched.
[/list]

[h1] Compatibility [/h1]
The mod adds new files only. It replaces no vanilla file. It is not tested against other mods that change children or pregnancy.
